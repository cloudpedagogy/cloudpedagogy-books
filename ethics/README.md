# Generative AI Ethics Collection  
**CloudPedagogy Open Books on Ethics, Meaning, and Governance**

This folder contains open-access books exploring ethical, cultural, and governance dimensions of generative artificial intelligence.

Published by **CloudPedagogy**, these books examine how generative AI reshapes knowledge, creativity, responsibility, and institutional practice across education, research, public service, and society.

---

## Overview

The Generative AI Ethics collection provides layered exploration of ethical issues associated with generative artificial intelligence systems, including large language models, image generators, and multimodal AI tools.

The books are designed to support:

- Conceptual understanding of AI ethics  
- Practical and institutional governance reflection  
- Professional and educational capability development  
- Responsible human–AI collaboration  

Together, the books combine accessibility with depth, allowing readers to engage with ethical issues at different levels of complexity.

---

## Books in This Collection

### Generative AI Ethics  
**Overview and Foundational Guide**

📁 `generative-ai-ethics/`

This book provides a structured, accessible introduction to the ethical, social, and governance challenges introduced by generative AI.

It explores:

- Ethical foundations of generative AI  
- Truth, misinformation, and epistemic risk  
- Bias, fairness, and representation  
- Privacy, data ownership, and consent  
- Labour, creativity, and human agency  
- Policy, regulation, and institutional responsibility  
- Global and cross-cultural perspectives  
- Ethical system design and public engagement  
- Futures of generative AI ethics  

This volume is designed as an **accessible entry point and broad reference text**.

---

### Generative AI Ethics: Meaning, Authorship, and Governance  
**Advanced Conceptual and Governance Exploration**

📁 `generative-ai-ethics-meaning-authorship-governance/`

This book provides a deeper and more reflective examination of generative AI ethics, focusing on cultural, philosophical, and governance implications.

It explores:

- Meaning-making and authorship in AI-assisted creation  
- Human responsibility and distributed agency  
- Data, provenance, and cultural extraction  
- Accountability across AI development and deployment pipelines  
- Institutional and regulatory ethics  
- Emotional, psychological, and relational impacts of AI  
- Power, equity, and global justice considerations  
- Ecological, posthuman, and regenerative ethics  

This volume is designed as a **conceptually deeper and more specialised companion text**.

---

## Relationship Between the Books

The books are intentionally complementary:

- **Generative AI Ethics**  
  → Broad overview and foundational ethics introduction  

- **Generative AI Ethics: Meaning, Authorship, and Governance**  
  → Extended conceptual, cultural, and governance-focused exploration  

Readers may begin with the overview volume and progress to the advanced text, or use either independently depending on their interests and professional context.
