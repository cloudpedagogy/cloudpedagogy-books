# Version History  
**Generative AI Ethics: Meaning, Authorship, and Governance**

This document records the public release history of *Generative AI Ethics: Meaning, Authorship, and Governance*, published by CloudPedagogy.

---

## Versioning Principles

- Versions reflect **substantive conceptual or structural changes**, not minor editorial fixes.
- Patch-level changes (e.g. typo corrections, formatting adjustments) may not always trigger a new public version.
- Each version represents a **stable, citable snapshot** of the work.

---

## v1.0  
**Initial public release**

This release establishes the complete conceptual and structural foundation of the book, including:

- Analysis of meaning, authorship, responsibility, and governance  
- Integrated treatment of ethics traditions, justice, care, and power  
- Institutional, educational, and policy-oriented perspectives  
- Forward-looking reflections on regenerative and posthuman ethics  

This version is intended as a stable reference point for citation, teaching, and further development within the CloudPedagogy ecosystem.
