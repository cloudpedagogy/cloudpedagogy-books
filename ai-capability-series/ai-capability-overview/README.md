# AI Capability: An Overview  
*A book in the CloudPedagogy AI Capability Series*

**Status:** Final (v1.0)

---

## Overview

**AI Capability: An Overview** is an open-access book developed by **CloudPedagogy** as part of the **AI Capability Series** — a structured set of governance-aware books designed to support responsible, effective, and sustainable use of artificial intelligence across education, research, and public service contexts.

This book provides a conceptual and practical introduction to **AI capability as a human, organisational, and governance challenge**, rather than a technical or tool-focused problem. It establishes the foundations needed to understand how AI reshapes judgement, responsibility, decision-making, and institutional practice.

The book is written for educators, researchers, professional staff, leaders, policymakers, and organisations seeking to engage with AI in ways that preserve agency, accountability, and long-term learning.

---

## Purpose of the Book

This book is designed to:

- Introduce AI capability as a **distinct form of institutional and professional capability**
- Establish a **shared language** for discussing human–AI work, decision-making, and responsibility
- Provide orientation to the **CloudPedagogy AI Capability Framework**
- Serve as an entry point to the wider **AI Capability Series**

Rather than offering tools, prompts, or technical guidance, the book focuses on **how AI is situated within human systems**, and why capability must be deliberately developed and renewed over time.

---

## Position in the AI Capability Series

The **AI Capability Series** is grounded in the **CloudPedagogy AI Capability Framework**, which defines six interdependent capability domains:

1. AI Awareness and Orientation  
2. Human–AI Co-Agency  
3. Applied Generative AI Practice  
4. Ethics, Equity, and Impact  
5. Decision-Making and Governance  
6. Reflection, Learning, and Renewal  

**AI Capability: An Overview** provides the conceptual foundation for the series. It explains why these domains exist, how they relate to one another, and why none of them can function effectively in isolation.

Readers may use this book as:
- A standalone introduction to AI capability  
- A reference point when engaging with domain-specific books  
- A shared framing text for discussion, training, or organisational planning  

---

## Scope and Approach

This book deliberately:

- Focuses on **capability rather than technology**
- Treats AI as a **socio-technical system embedded in human contexts**
- Emphasises **judgement, responsibility, and governance**
- Avoids vendor-, model-, or tool-specific guidance  

The approach is:

- Conceptually rigorous but accessible  
- Grounded in real institutional practice  
- Suitable for individual reading, facilitated discussion, or professional development  

---

## Structure (Indicative)

The book is structured to introduce AI capability as a coherent system rather than a checklist. Core sections include:

- The purpose and framing of AI capability  
- The six capability domains and how they interact  
- Common risks of fragmented or tool-driven AI adoption  
- The role of reflection and renewal in sustaining capability  

This overview is intended to be returned to over time, not read once and set aside.

## Intended Audience

This book is written for:

- Educators and academic staff  
- Learning technologists and digital education leads

- Professional services and operational leaders
