# Applied Generative AI Practice  
*A book in the CloudPedagogy AI Capability Series*

**Status:** Final (v1.0)

---

## Overview

**Applied Generative AI Practice** is an open-access book published by **CloudPedagogy** as part of the **AI Capability Series**. The book focuses on how generative AI is integrated into **real work**, not as a technical novelty but as an organisational and professional practice shaped by judgement, constraints, and accountability.

Rather than explaining tools, models, or prompts, this book examines **what it means to use generative AI well in practice**—across education, research, and organisational contexts—while remaining aligned with ethical principles, governance structures, and human decision-making.

The central premise of the book is that **capability resides in practice**, not in technology. Generative AI becomes valuable only when it is embedded thoughtfully into workflows that preserve human agency, support learning, and remain open to review and renewal.

---

## Purpose of the Book

This book is designed to:

- Reframe generative AI use as a **practice capability**, not a technical skill  
- Clarify where generative AI adds value and where it introduces risk  
- Show how practice develops over time—from experimentation to maturity  
- Surface common misalignments such as tool-driven use or undocumented practice  
- Support reflective, governance-aligned use of AI in everyday work  

It is written for readers who are already using—or are expected to use—generative AI, and who need **conceptual clarity and practical framing**, not tutorials.

## Scope and Deliberate Boundaries

### What This Book Focuses On

The book focuses on:

- Sense-making, drafting, synthesis, and exploration  
- Workflow integration under real-world constraints  
- Human review, interpretation, and oversight  
- Documentation, learning, and practice renewal  

Practice is treated as **situated, imperfect, and evolving**, not as an idealised end state.

### What This Book Deliberately Avoids

This book **does not** provide:

- Tool comparisons or vendor recommendations  
- Prompt libraries or optimisation techniques  
- Model architectures or technical explanations  
- Claims about productivity gains divorced from context  

These topics are intentionally excluded to prevent practice from being reduced to **tool proficiency** rather than capability.

---

## Structure (Indicative)

The book is organised around:

- Defining applied generative AI practice clearly  
- Identifying common practice patterns and boundaries  
- Exploring how practice capability develops over time  
- Examining risks and misalignment in real use  
- Integrating practice with co-agency, ethics, and governance  
- Applying practice across education, research, and organisations  
- Supporting reflection and renewal as tools and contexts change  

Throughout, the emphasis remains on **how AI is actually used**, not how it is marketed.

---

## Intended Audience

This book is written for:

- Educators and learning designers  
- Researchers and research support professionals  
- Learning technologists and digital specialists  
- Professional services and operational teams  
- Managers and leaders overseeing AI use  

It assumes no advanced technical knowledge, but does assume **active engagement with AI-supported work**.
