# AI Capability Series  
**Books for Human–AI Understanding, Practice, and Governance**

An open-access book series published by **CloudPedagogy**.

---

## Overview

The **AI Capability Series** is a collection of interconnected books exploring what it means to develop, exercise, and govern capability in an age of artificial intelligence.

Rather than treating AI as a set of tools or technologies in isolation, the series approaches AI as a **human capability challenge**—one that involves awareness, judgment, ethical responsibility, institutional governance, and continuous learning. Each book focuses on a distinct dimension of AI capability, while together they form a coherent and comprehensive framework for responsible human–AI engagement.

The series is written for educators, researchers, policymakers, learning designers, digital leaders, and institutions seeking to move beyond surface-level AI literacy toward deeper, more durable forms of capability.

---

## Conceptual Orientation

Across the series, AI capability is understood as:

- **Human-centred**, not automation-driven  
- **Contextual and situational**, not generic or tool-specific  
- **Ethically grounded**, not compliance-only  
- **Governance-aware**, not individualised  
- **Developmental and reflective**, not static  

The books emphasise human–AI co-agency, decision-making under uncertainty, and the institutional conditions required for responsible AI use in education, research, and public service.

---

## Books in the Series

Each book can be read independently, but they are designed to be mutually reinforcing.

### AI Capability: An Overview  
Introduces the core concept of AI capability and the six-domain framework that underpins the series. Establishes shared language, principles, and orientation.

### Human–AI Co-Agency  
Explores partnership, responsibility, and role clarity in human–AI collaboration. Examines how agency is distributed across people, systems, and institutions.

### Applied Generative AI Practice  
Focuses on practical engagement with generative AI, including design patterns, creative use, and innovation grounded in human judgment and ethical awareness.

### Ethics, Equity, and Impact  
Examines ethical traditions, justice, care, power, and social consequences of AI systems. Addresses bias, representation, consent, and harm.

### Decision-Making and Governance  
Addresses institutional governance, accountability, policy, and decision-making in AI-enabled environments. Focuses on boards, leaders, and organisational systems.

### Reflection, Learning, and Renewal  
Emphasises reflection, evaluation, and ongoing capability development. Explores how individuals and institutions learn with AI over time.

## How to Use the Series

The books may be used:

- As **standalone references** for specific domains of AI capability  
- As a **structured reading sequence** aligned to the CloudPedagogy AI Capability Framework  
- As **course texts or CPD materials** in higher education and professional contexts  
- As **governance resources** for institutional policy, strategy, and review  

A common pathway through the series is:

**Orient → Apply → Govern → Reflect → Renew**
