# Version History  
**Text-Based Generative AI: Concepts, Capabilities, Risks, and Responsible Use**

This document records public releases and substantive updates to *Text-Based Generative AI: Concepts, Capabilities, Risks, and Responsible Use*.

The book is published as a living text. Revisions may include clarification, expansion, correction, or reorganisation of content in response to developments in generative AI, emerging tools, institutional practice, and responsible AI governance.

## Versioning Approach

Version numbers follow semantic versioning principles:

- **Major version changes** reflect significant conceptual or structural revision  
- **Minor version changes** reflect content expansion or notable clarification  
- **Patch updates** reflect small corrections or editorial improvements
