# Text-Based Generative AI  
**Concepts, Capabilities, Risks, and Responsible Use**

An open-access book by **Jonathan Wong**, published by **CloudPedagogy**.

---

## Overview

*Text-Based Generative AI: Concepts, Capabilities, Risks, and Responsible Use* provides a comprehensive and accessible exploration of text-based generative artificial intelligence, including large language models and conversational AI systems.

The book introduces how these technologies work, how they are applied across professional and educational contexts, and how they can be used responsibly and ethically. It combines conceptual explanation, practical guidance, and governance-oriented reflection to support informed and thoughtful engagement with generative AI.

The book is written for educators, researchers, policymakers, professionals, students, and institutions seeking to build durable capability in working with text-based AI systems.

This book forms part of the wider **CloudPedagogy** ecosystem of open, governance-aware resources supporting responsible AI capability and human–AI collaboration.

---

## Scope and Themes

The book explores:

- Foundations of text-based generative AI and large language models  
- Capabilities and limitations of conversational AI systems  
- Practical applications across education, research, and professional work  
- Prompt design and interaction strategies  
- Opportunities and productivity gains  
- Risks, errors, and systemic limitations  
- Responsible and ethical use of AI systems  
- Technical foundations for advanced readers  
- Emerging future directions in generative AI  
- Practical resources for continued capability development  

---

## Structure (Table of Contents)

### Core Content

1. **Introduction**  
   Establishes the importance, scope, and societal context of text-based generative AI.

2. **Foundations of Text-Based Generative AI**  
   Explains how large language models operate, including training processes, architecture, and key conceptual principles.

3. **Key Text-Based Generative AI Tools**  
   Surveys major categories of tools and platforms used in generative text production.

4. **Applications of Text-Based Generative AI**  
   Explores real-world use cases across education, research, communication, content development, and knowledge work.

5. **Prompting and Interaction Strategies**  
   Provides practical techniques for designing effective prompts and collaborating productively with AI systems.

6. **Benefits and Opportunities**  
   Examines productivity, creativity, accessibility, and innovation potential enabled by generative AI.

7. **Risks, Challenges, and Limitations**  
   Addresses hallucination, bias, misinformation, over-reliance, and structural system limitations.

8. **Responsible and Ethical Use**  
   Explores transparency, accountability, fairness, and human oversight in AI-supported workflows.

9. **Technical Deep Dive (Optional Advanced Section)**  
   Provides additional technical explanation for readers seeking deeper understanding of model behaviour and system design.

10. **Future Directions of Text-Based Generative AI**  
    Examines emerging developments, multimodal integration, and long-term implications.

11. **Practical Resources**  
    Offers tools, reflection prompts, and guidance for continued learning and responsible adoption.
