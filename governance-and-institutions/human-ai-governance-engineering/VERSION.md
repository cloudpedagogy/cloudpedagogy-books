# Version History

## v1.0 — March 2026

First public release of *Human–AI Governance Engineering*.

This edition introduces the conceptual foundations of the discipline of Human–AI Governance Engineering, including:

- the Human–AI Governance Stack
- the Human–AI Decision Cycle
- five design principles for governance-ready systems
- seven failure modes of human–AI decision systems
- six professional roles in human–AI decision environments
- principles for designing inspectable decision systems

The book argues that the central challenge of artificial intelligence is institutional: designing human systems capable of governing hybrid intelligence responsibly.
