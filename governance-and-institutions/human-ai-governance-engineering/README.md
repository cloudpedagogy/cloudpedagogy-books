## Overview

*Human–AI Governance Engineering* introduces a new discipline focused on designing responsible decision systems where human judgement and machine intelligence interact.

As artificial intelligence becomes embedded in professional environments, institutions increasingly rely on hybrid intelligence systems combining human expertise with machine-generated analysis. This book explores how such systems can remain transparent, accountable, and governable.

The work develops a conceptual framework for Human–AI Governance Engineering, including:

- the **Human–AI Decision Cycle**
- the **Human–AI Governance Stack**
- design principles for governance-ready workflows
- common failure modes of human–AI decision systems
- the emerging role of **governance engineers**

The central argument of the book is that the real challenge of artificial intelligence is not simply technological. The deeper challenge is institutional: designing decision systems that harness machine intelligence while preserving human responsibility.
