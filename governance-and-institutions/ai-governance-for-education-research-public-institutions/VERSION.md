# Version History
AI Governance for Education, Research, and Public Institutions

This document records the public release history of *AI Governance for Education, Research, and Public Institutions*, published by CloudPedagogy.

The canonical version of the book is maintained in Markdown format.  
PDF editions are generated from the Markdown source for reading and citation.

---

## Versioning Principles

- Versions reflect **substantive conceptual, structural, or implementation-oriented changes**, not minor editorial fixes.
- Patch-level corrections (e.g. typographical errors, formatting refinements, or layout adjustments) may not always trigger a new public version.
- Each version represents a **stable, citable snapshot** of the work.
- The book is maintained as a **living governance implementation reference**, evolving alongside institutional AI practice and capability development.

## v1.0 — Initial Public Release  
**Status:** Stable  
**Release Type:** Foundational Edition

This release establishes the full conceptual, strategic, and operational foundation of institutional AI governance, including:

### Core Governance Foundations
- Transition from individual AI responsibility to institutional governance accountability  
- Organisational control environments for AI adoption  
- Governance operating model design  

### Institutional Governance Architecture
- AI governance charters and committee structures  
- RACI and distributed accountability models  
- Policy stack design for institutional AI use  
- Risk tiering and assurance framework development  

### Implementation and Operational Practice
- Procurement and vendor governance approaches  
- Institutional rollout and change management playbooks  
- Incident governance and accountability frameworks  
- Monitoring, reporting, and governance performance metrics  
- Governance maturity models and institutional roadmapping  

### Applied Institutional Case Patterns
- Higher education governance implementation models  
- Research governance integration approaches  
- Public sector and cross-sector governance coordination patterns  

### Governance Toolkit and Implementation Resources
- Governance charter templates  
- Committee terms of reference models  
- Risk registers and impact assessment structures  
- Model documentation and assurance artefact examples  
- Institutional governance onboarding and implementation guidance  

### Institutional Governance Appendices
- Governance Quick-Start Implementation Guide  
- Governance Maturity Self-Assessment Diagnostic  
- Governance Artefact Lifecycle Mapping Framework  
- Governance Glossary and Template Reference Library  
- Implementation Checklists and Operational Readiness Guidance  

This version is intended as a **practical institutional governance reference manual** supporting leadership, governance bodies, compliance teams, and operational AI implementation teams across education, research, and public-sector environments.
