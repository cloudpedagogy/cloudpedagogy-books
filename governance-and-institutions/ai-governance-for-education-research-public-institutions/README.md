
# AI Governance for Education, Research, and Public Institutions

**An open-access book by Jonathan Wong, published by CloudPedagogy.**

---

## Overview

*AI Governance for Education, Research, and Public Institutions* provides a comprehensive, implementation-focused guide to designing, operating, and sustaining institutional governance for artificial intelligence.

As organisations move from experimentation with AI to large-scale operational adoption, governance becomes essential for ensuring transparency, accountability, ethical responsibility, and institutional trust. This book explores how institutions can build governance systems that support innovation while managing risk, compliance, and public responsibility.

The book combines governance theory, organisational design principles, practical implementation playbooks, and reusable governance templates. It is written for institutional leaders, governance and risk professionals, educators, researchers, policy teams, digital transformation leaders, and public-sector organisations responsible for overseeing AI-supported decision-making.

This book forms part of the wider CloudPedagogy ecosystem of open, governance-aware resources supporting responsible AI capability, institutional stewardship, and human–AI collaboration.

---

## Scope and Themes

The book explores:

- Institutional operating models for AI governance  
- Governance charters, committee structures, and RACI accountability models  
- AI policy stack design across teaching, research, procurement, and data governance  
- Risk tiering and assurance review frameworks  
- Audit readiness and regulatory alignment strategies  
- Vendor and procurement governance for AI systems  
- Institutional rollout and change management playbooks  
- Incident governance and accountability frameworks  
- Governance metrics, dashboards, and reporting models  
- Governance maturity and capability roadmapping  
- Cross-sector governance case patterns  
- Implementation toolkits, templates, and diagnostic instruments

## Structure (Table of Contents)

### Core Content

**Preface: Why Institutional AI Governance Matters Now**  
Explores the shift from individual AI experimentation to organisation-wide adoption and the need for structured governance capability.

---

### Chapters

**Chapter 1 — From Capability to Institutional Responsibility**  
Examines why governance cannot rely solely on individual ethical decision-making and introduces governance as an organisational capability.

**Chapter 2 — Models of AI Governance**  
Explores centralised, federated, risk-tiered, and human-in-the-loop governance models, including governance maturity trajectories.

**Chapter 3 — The Institutional Control Environment**  
Examines governance charters, oversight committees, lines of defence frameworks, and institutional accountability design.

**Chapter 4 — Building an AI Policy Stack**  
Provides guidance on acceptable use, academic integrity, research governance, procurement governance, data governance, and incident response policies.

**Chapter 5 — Risk Tiering and Assurance Frameworks**  
Introduces impact-based risk classification, ethical assurance review processes, and proportionate governance approaches.

**Chapter 6 — Evidence, Audit, and Regulatory Alignment**  
Explores governance documentation standards, audit trail design, transparency reporting, and external assurance readiness.

**Chapter 7 — Procurement and Vendor Governance**  
Examines supplier due diligence, model risk evaluation, contractual governance controls, and third-party transparency expectations.

**Chapter 8 — Institutional Rollout Playbooks**  
Provides phased adoption models from pilot to sustained operational governance, including change management strategies.

**Chapter 9 — Incident Governance and Accountability**  
Explores institutional response to AI misuse, model failure, escalation pathways, and post-incident learning frameworks.

**Chapter 10 — Metrics, Dashboards, and Governance Reporting**  
Introduces governance KPIs, monitoring dashboards, institutional reporting cycles, and evidence-informed review processes.

**Chapter 11 — Maturity Models and Roadmapping**  
Provides governance capability benchmarking approaches and continuous improvement planning.

**Chapter 12 — Governance Case Patterns**  
Examines governance models across higher education, research organisations, public sector bodies, and cross-sector collaborations.

**Chapter 13 — Template and Implementation Toolkit**  
Provides reusable governance artefacts including charters, committee TORs, risk registers, DPIA examples, and model documentation templates.

---

### Epilogue

**Towards Sustainable and Trustworthy Institutional AI**  
Explores governance as cultural infrastructure, the role of human judgement, and the future of institutional AI stewardship.

---

### Appendices

**Appendix A — Governance Quick-Start Guide**  
Institutional first-steps playbook for launching AI governance programmes.

**Appendix B — Governance Maturity Self-Assessment**  
Capability diagnostic framework for evaluating institutional AI governance readiness.

**Appendix C — Governance Artefact Lifecycle Map**  
Implementation navigation framework mapping governance artefacts across AI adoption stages.

**Appendix D — Glossary**  
Definitions of governance, assurance, risk, and AI oversight terminology.

**Appendix E — Governance Template Pack References**  
Catalogue of reusable governance artefacts and documentation templates.

**Appendix F — Implementation Checklists**  
Operational checklists supporting governance rollout, monitoring, and review.
